# R-460 Poseidon 1.5.3

Major visual overhaul, 2079 modernization, and encyclopedia fix for the R-460 Poseidon missile family.

### Visual Overhaul & 2079 Modernization
- **Refined Aerodynamic Fuselage**: Re-engineered supersonic aerodynamic profile (0.49 m diameter) eliminating the bulky profile while preserving aerodynamic handling.
- **Articulating Vectoring Nozzle**: 16-petal thrust vectoring nozzle with realistic titanium heat bloom and maintenance access panel.
- **3D Combustion Chamber & Turbine**: Fully modeled interior with closed turbine bulkhead, 24 stator blades, concentric afterburner spray rings, 8 guide vanes, and central tail bullet cone.
- **Dual Aluminum Nose Thrusters**: Symmetrical attitude control thruster pods on both starboard (+X) and port (-X) forward radome flanks.
- **Masterwork 2K PBR Textures**: Tactical naval anthracite finish, calibrated dark PBR metallic/smoothness (fixing previous washed-out engine reflections in Unity), and English technical military stencils (R-460 POSEIDON, NO STEP).

### Fixes
- **R-460N Tactical Nuclear Encyclopedia & Cost**: Fixed unit definition reference in R_460_Poseidon_Nuclear.prefab so the in-game Encyclopedia accurately reports the 34 million cost, nuclear anti-ship role, and 1.5 kt yield instead of falling back to the conventional missile.

Install R-460 Poseidon_1.5.3.nobp and PoseidonTELRuntime.dll together. Remove older Poseidon .nobp files before installing. Nuclear Option 0.34.2, BepInEx, and NOBlueprinter are required.
