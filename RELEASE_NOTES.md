# R-460 Poseidon 1.4.1

Both missile variants now cost 3 million instead of 30 million. Radar size is reduced from 0.01 to 0.008. Armor tier rises from 0.1 to 0.12, damage tolerance from 30 to 35, and pierce/blast armor from 10 to 12.

Install `R-460 Poseidon_1.4.1.nobp` and `PoseidonTELRuntime.dll` together. Remove older Poseidon `.nobp` files before installing. Nuclear Option 0.34.2, BepInEx, and NOBlueprinter are required.
